# Restart pre-funding Session failures as new orders

When a membership order's Session is unusable before any financial exposure, KaWang terminally cancels that order and fulfillment, restores the CDK to `active`, and creates a new order on the player's next submission. We prefer an append-only attempt history over replacing the protected Session or reviving the old order. Financial exposure begins at the earliest of card reservation, an open-card or funding request, release of full card material, activation of a possibly authorizing checkout control, or appearance of a related card transaction. After that boundary, release is forbidden: the original fulfillment retains its CDK and financial evidence, accepts only a replacement Session proven to belong to the same ChatGPT identity, and reconciles membership and card transactions before another payment can be considered.
