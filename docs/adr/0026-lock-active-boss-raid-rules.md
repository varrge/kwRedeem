# Lock Active Boss Raid Rules

Boss health, the effective-raider threshold, theme damage multipliers, rewards, and schedule become immutable when a Raid Month starts. Unlike the forward-versioned Shake & Win configuration, these values define a shared competitive ranking and a published collective target; changing them during play would alter the value of already-recorded damage and undermine both winner selection and the pre-approved reward budget. Administrators may end a campaign early and may clone its configuration for a later month, but cannot revise the active competition.
