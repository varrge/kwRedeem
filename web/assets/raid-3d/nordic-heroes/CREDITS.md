# Medieval adventurer attribution

These models are adaptations of the following works by **Clement Wu, Nikolaus & Botanic** (submitted by Botanic on OpenGameArt):

- `knight.glb`: [Animated Knight](https://opengameart.org/content/animated-knight). Sources: [knight.blend](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/knight.blend), [Knight.png](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Knight_1.png).
- `ranger.glb`: [Animated Archer](https://opengameart.org/content/animated-archer). Sources: [archer_version_3.blend](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/archer_version_3.blend), [Archer.png](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Archer.png).
- `mage.glb`: [Animated Mage](https://opengameart.org/content/animated-mage). Sources: [mage.blend](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/mage.blend), [Mage.png](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Mage.png).

Modifications for kwRedeem: converted the original Blender rigs and textures into self-contained glTF 2.0 binaries; baked the original bone constraints; retained Idle, Attack and Hit animations (renamed knight Damaged to Hit); normalized the idle height to 2 units; reduced texture saturation and brightness for the Nordic courtyard; converted the original diffuse materials to rough PBR materials; removed the mage's six broad bottom hem flaps. Body proportions and original character designs are retained. No Bethesda/Skyrim game assets are included.
