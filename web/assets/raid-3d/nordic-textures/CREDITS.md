- https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/cobblestone_floor_02/cobblestone_floor_02_diff_1k.jpg

Adaptation: resized to 512×512 using Lanczos and recompressed at JPEG quality82. Local files `stone.jpg` and `paving.jpg` retain the original texture colors. The renderer adds material tint, roughness and a subtle luminance bump. No normals/displacement maps are claimed. The procedural fallback and scene geometry are original project work.
