# 亡骸主宰 — Zombie — Fully Animated

- Artist: **Cethiel**

The original authored 60fps PNG sprite sheets have fixed 384×288 cells. Empty trailing slots are removed. Idle Battle / Intimidation / Hurt / Death are used for idle / battle / hurt / death, sampled every 6 / 3 / 2 / 4 frames, always preserving the final nonempty frame. The shared crop `(111,35,375,242)` yields a 264×207 frame, six columns per WebP atlas. Colors and alpha are retained; the webpage brightens the dark art. No per-frame recentering. All animation and artwork are by Cethiel; KaWang only samples, crops and packs them. Death is shortened to2.4s and holds its final visible corpse pose.

Frame counts, timing and source files are recorded in `metadata.json`. This is the lower-resolution source provided by the author; it is not advertised as HD artwork.
